- 2026-11-26: G2 v2 diagnosis. Decoys get reads far less often than target nulls: 1509 decoy hits / 1.13M decoy nulls (0.13%) vs 3780 / 224k target nulls (1.7%); median reads 2 vs 10, breadth 0.07 vs 0.27. Target-null hits are real homolog reads (median read identity 0.85; 3384 of 3780 below 0.90), concentrated in 144 units, mostly intrinsic chromosomal efflux/regulator genes (YojI, Ecol_mdfA, MexB, acrD, TolC, AcrF, cpxA, mdtC, smeE, MuxC, sepA, emrR, H-NS). Cause is structural: a decoy is its parent plus mutations, so reads from a homolog of the parent map best to the parent, not to the decoy (85.5% of decoy hits have the parent hit in the same sample). Decoys derived from targets therefore cannot mimic homolog nulls in a competitive index, whatever their identity profile. Checks that also FAIL: (a) score trained with target nulls as negatives (99% tail 478 vs 3.2; results/g2v2/gates_score_trained_with_target_nulls.json); (b) targets restricted by CARD Resistance Mechanism (src/pilot/g2_by_mechanism.py): no efflux 221 vs 0.8, inactivation only 44 vs 0.2. 179 target-null hits have read identity >=0.95, but all with low breadth (~3%), i.e. short conserved stretches under the 150-bp fragment rule, not label misses. Also note: a read-identity filter >=0.90 would remove most homolog hits but, by construction, all decoy hits (decoy windows <0.90). → Target-decoy calibration is not valid for ARG detection in this setting; the failure (uniform and homolog-structured decoys) is a reportable negative result. Decision pending (researcher + supervisor): calibration-null source for the main method.
